05 / PARENTS, WE’VE GOT YOU.

NEW TO LAX?
START HERE.

First season or fifth, a little clarity goes a long way.

Still have a question? Let’s talk ?
Does my child need prior experience to play?
No! Players of all skill levels—including complete beginners—are welcome. Teams are organized by grade and experience level.
How do I register and which program do I select?
Go to www.imlaxutah.org, click Register, and select Roy Youth as your program. You will also be prompted to get/link an annual USA Lacrosse youth membership ($35/yr) for player insurance.
Do we have to buy all new equipment right away?
No. For boys, IMLAX offers a full-season gear rental (helmet, shoulder pads, arm pads, gloves) for $85 starting January 25, 2027. You only need to supply a stick, cleats, mouthguard, and athletic cup.
When and where are practices and games?
Practices start on or after February 24, 2027, usually twice a week at local Roy fields. Games are played on Saturdays starting March 20, 2027 against other Utah community teams.
Are scholarships available?
Yes, IMLAX offers financial aid/scholarships. Applications are due Monday, February 1, 2027 through the IMLAX website.
How can my child practice at home?
“Wall ball”—10 minutes a day throwing and catching against a brick or concrete wall (or rebounder) using both right and left hands—is the fastest way to build stick skills.
